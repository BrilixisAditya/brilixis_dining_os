# Brilixis Restaurant Operating System

## Vision

Restaurant Operating System (ROS) is a multi-vendor SaaS platform that enables restaurants to manage their complete day-to-day operations from a single system.

The platform provides QR-code based customer ordering, waiter management, kitchen operations, billing, payments, analytics, and business insights.

The goal is to replace manual restaurant workflows with a real-time digital platform.
